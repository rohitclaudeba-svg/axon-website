# Approved Keyword / Intent Clusters

Search topics the content is written around. These are intent clusters, not
a target for keyword density — copy is written for patients and families
first (spec §16). Do not add pages purely to target a keyword without
genuine content behind them.

## Service Clusters

- Speech therapy — communication, language delay, swallowing difficulties
- Occupational therapy — fine motor skills, sensory processing, daily living independence
- Physiotherapy — pain, mobility, post-injury/post-surgical recovery
- Special education — individualised learning, learning difficulties

## Program Clusters

- Pediatric rehabilitation — child development, multidisciplinary pediatric care
- Neurological rehabilitation — stroke recovery, TBI, Parkinson's, MS
- Orthopedic & musculoskeletal rehabilitation — fractures, sports injury, post-surgical
- Geriatric rehabilitation — balance/fall risk, healthy ageing, independence

## Condition Clusters

Mirrors `frontend/content/conditions.ts` grouping:

- Developmental & learning (ASD, ADHD, Cerebral Palsy, Down Syndrome, learning difficulties)
- Neurological (stroke, TBI, Parkinson's, MS, Guillain-Barré, neuropathy, ataxia)
- Orthopedic & musculoskeletal (fractures, sports injury, arthritis, joint replacement)
- Geriatric & age-related (fall risk, dementia, post-hip-replacement)

## Local/GEO Clusters

- "[service] near me" / "[service] in [city]" — supported once real
  location data is approved (spec never invents a location)
- "multi-specialty rehabilitation centre" / "rehabilitation centre under one roof"
- "book physiotherapy/speech therapy appointment"

## Explicitly Avoided

- Outcome or cure claims ("guaranteed results", "cured")
- Superlatives not backed by supplied facts ("best", "#1", "award-winning")
- Doorway pages combining every service × every possible location
